Demo media credits

The Home and detail screenshots are real captures of Pleya 2.8.1 build 315 on Mac, iPhone and Apple TV, built from release commit 5bf8573c. Liquid Glass is enabled on iPhone and Apple TV; the app does not offer that mode on Mac. Capture provenance is recorded in /media/capture-manifest.json. Their two demo collections contain twelve fictional films with original generated posters and backdrops created for Pleya. Source artwork is recorded in /media/catalog.json. No commercial film posters were used for these new screenshots.
