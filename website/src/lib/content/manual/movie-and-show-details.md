# Movie and show details

Tapping a title opens its detail page: a large backdrop, the title or its logo, rating,
year and summary, then seasons and episodes for a show, then cast, trailers and extras,
and finally more like this.

![A show detail page](/docs-media/detail-show.png)

## The action buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play** / **Resume** | Starts playback. For a show it picks the right next episode. |
| **Trailer** | Plays the trailer |
| **Shuffle** | Plays episodes in random order |
| **Download** | Saves it for offline, turning into a progress ring while it runs |
| **Watched tick** | Marks the title watched or unwatched |
| **Watchlist** | Adds or removes it, films and shows only, online only |
| **More** | Opens the full menu |

On a narrow screen some of these move into the **More** menu.

## Seasons and episodes

Every episode shows a thumbnail, its number, title, a short description and a red progress
bar when you are part way through. Tap to play, or to open details, whichever you set in
Settings. The season picker switches seasons.

![The episode list with one episode in progress](/docs-media/detail-episodes.png)

If you would rather not read what happens next, turn on **Hide spoilers for unwatched
episodes**. Thumbnails and descriptions of episodes you have not seen are blurred until you
have.

## Versions

A title can exist on the server in more than one quality, for example a 4K file and a 1080p
one. Pleya picks one to start with, and you can switch during playback through **Version &
quality** in the player.

## Extras

Trailers, deleted scenes and behind-the-scenes material appear as their own row when the
server has them. They play in the same player as everything else.
