# Downloads and offline

Films and episodes can be stored on the device and watched without a connection.

## Downloading

1. Open the quick menu of a title (long-press) or its detail page.
2. Choose **Download**.
3. For a show, pick **all episodes** or **unwatched only**, and whether specials come along.
4. Follow it on the detail button (a percentage) or in the **Downloads** tab.

![The downloads tab with one download in progress](/docs-media/downloads-manage.png)

## The Downloads tab

Three tabs: **Manage**, **TV shows** and **Movies**. From Manage you can pause everything,
resume everything, delete everything, or handle one item at a time.

Downloads resume by themselves after the system pauses them, after the network drops, and
on a retry. A queue that looks stuck usually is not: check whether **Download on Wi-Fi
only** is on and you are on mobile data.

## Sync rules

A show can carry a standing rule, such as **keep synced** or "keep three unwatched episodes
ready". New episodes then download by themselves and watched ones can be cleaned up
automatically. Rules live under **Sync rules** in the Downloads tab.

![A sync rule set on a show](/docs-media/sync-rules.png)

## Watching offline

With no connection, Pleya opens on the Downloads tab and everything you saved plays as
normal. Your position is stored locally and pushed back to the server the moment you are
online again. If another device watched further in the meantime, the further position wins
rather than the most recent write.

Downloads are per device. Saving something on your phone does not put it on your tablet.

## Download settings

- **Download on Wi-Fi only** keeps downloads off your mobile data
- **Auto-remove watched downloads** clears finished items by itself
- **Download location** is inside the app by default, or a folder you pick on a computer
