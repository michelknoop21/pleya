# FAQ

## Does Pleya come with any content?

No. It plays what is on your own Plex or Jellyfin server, or on one somebody shared with
you.

## Can I use Plex and Jellyfin at the same time?

Yes. Connect both and the content merges on the home screen and in search.

## Why does a title show something like "87% match"?

That is the title's rating expressed as a percentage. It is not a personal prediction, and
it is the same number for every profile.

## Does everyone in the house share one history?

Only if you let them. Give each person a [profile](/docs/profiles) and they each get their
own Continue watching row and history.

## How do I keep my children out of my profile?

Put a four-digit PIN on it.

## Why do I not see the Live TV tab?

It only appears when a connected server has a tuner or DVR.

## Can I cast to a Chromecast?

No. Install Pleya on the television itself (Apple TV or Android TV), or use the
[Companion Remote](/docs/apple-tv-and-remotes) to drive the TV app from your phone.

## Do downloads work across my devices?

No, they are per device. Download on the device you want to watch on.

## The picture is blurry or blocky. How do I get better quality?

Raise **Default quality**, or set it to Original. Higher quality asks more of your
connection and of the server.

## Can I skip intros automatically?

Yes, turn on **Auto skip intro**. It acts on episodes, not on films, where a chapter
marked as an intro tends to be part of the picture. Otherwise a button appears.

## How do I change the language?

**Settings**, then **Appearance**, then **Language**. Pleya ships in English plus fourteen
translations.

## Subtitles do not line up with the picture.

Open the player settings, then **Subtitle sync**, and shift until they match.

## Can I see what is new on the server?

Yes, the **Recently added** row on Home.

## What happens if two people watch at the same time?

That is fine. How many simultaneous streams are possible is up to the server.

## Are the recommendations private?

Yes. Your taste profile is built on the device and stays there. Nothing about what you watch
is uploaded.

## Is Pleya open source?

Yes, under the GPL-3.0. It is a fork of [Plezy](https://github.com/edde746/plezy) by
edde746. The full licence and third-party notices ship with the app.
