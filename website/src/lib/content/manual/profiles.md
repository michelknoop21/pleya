# Profiles

Everyone in the house gets their own profile, with their own watch history, their own
**Continue watching** row and their own lists. Without profiles, one person finishing a
series wipes it off everybody else's home screen.

![The profile picker with three avatars](/docs-media/profile-picker.png)

## Picking a profile at launch

With more than one profile, Pleya opens on **Who's watching?**, a grid of avatars. Tap
yours and you continue where you were. A button at the bottom manages the list.

Do not want that screen every time? Turn off **Ask for profile on app open** in
[Settings](/docs/settings-reference). Pleya then opens with the profile you used last.

## Two kinds of profile

| Kind | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Plex Home profile** | Created automatically for every Home user on your Plex account |
| **Local profile** | You make it in Pleya: **New profile**, a name, and optionally a PIN |

A Plex Home profile keeps its history on the Plex side, so it follows you to other Plex
clients. A local profile lives in Pleya.

## PINs

A profile can be locked with a four-digit PIN. Switching to it asks for the code. This is
the usual way to keep children out of an adult profile, and it is the only lock Pleya has:
it protects the profile, not the app.

## Managing profiles

The profile overview shows the avatar, the name, an **Active** label on the current one and
a padlock where a PIN is set. Per profile you can manage it, **Remove** it, or **Sign out**
of it when it is a Plex profile.

On a phone the way in is [My Pleya](/docs/watchlist-and-my-pleya). On desktop and Apple TV
it is in the navigation.
