# Watch Together

Watch Together keeps playback in step between people in different places. One person
pauses, everyone pauses.

![A Watch Together session with two participants](/docs-media/watch-together.png)

## Starting a session

1. Open **Watch Together** from the icon on the home screen.
2. Choose **Create session**. You get a five-character code.
3. Share the code. The other person chooses **Join session** and enters it.
4. Start the film. Play, pause and seek stay in step for everyone.

## Who is allowed to control it

The person who created the session decides: **Host only**, or **Anyone**. Participants see
each other's presence. Leaving is always possible with **Leave session**, and the host can
close it with **End session**.

## What you need

Everyone needs their own access to the same title, on the same server or as their own copy.
Watch Together synchronises playback positions, not video: nothing is streamed between
participants.

## What goes over the network

Only playback sync messages, through a small relay. Nothing about your server, your library
or your account is exchanged, and the relay never sees any video.
