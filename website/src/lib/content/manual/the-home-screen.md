# The home screen

Home is the shop window for your own library: one large featured title at the top and rows
of posters underneath.

![The home billboard on Apple TV](/docs-media/home-hero.png)

## What is on it

- **The billboard.** A large featured item that changes every eight seconds, with a title,
  a description and buttons to start it. Half-watched titles carry a red progress bar.
- **Continue watching.** Everything you started and did not finish.
- **Next up.** The next episode of the series you are in the middle of.
- **Recently added.** New on the server.
- **Top 10.** The most-played titles with large rank numbers beside the posters, when your
  server publishes such a list.
- **Recommended for you**, **Because you watched X** and **Hidden gems**, built on this
  device from what you watched. Nothing about your taste is uploaded, and there is a switch
  in Settings to turn the rows off.

Hover a row title (desktop) or navigate to it and choose **See all** to open the whole row.

![Continue watching and a Top 10 row](/docs-media/home-rows.png)

## The match percentage

The green percentage next to a featured title (for example *87% match*) is the title's
rating expressed as a percentage. It is not a personal prediction and it does not change
between profiles.

## Navigation

| Device | How you move around |
|---|---|
| Phone and tablet | A bar at the bottom: Home, Media, Live TV, Search, My Pleya. Long-press **Media** to jump to another library. |
| Computer | A bar at the top with the Pleya wordmark and the same destinations as text. |
| Television | A sidebar on the left that expands, driven by the remote. |

A small cluster of faces appears in the toolbar while other people are streaming from your
server, if you have connected [Tautulli](/docs/movie-and-show-details). On Apple TV that is
a sidebar entry instead, because an overlay hanging under a toolbar button cannot be reached
with a remote.

**Live TV** only appears when a connected server offers it. Settings live inside
[My Pleya](/docs/watchlist-and-my-pleya) on a phone, and in the navigation on desktop and
TV.

## Cards and the quick menu

Every poster responds to three things:

- **Tap or click** opens the detail page, or plays straight away if you set it that way.
- **Hover** on a computer expands the card into a preview with round buttons after a short
  pause: play, add to your list, open details.
- **Long-press or right-click** opens the full menu.

| Menu item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play** | Starts the title, resuming where you were |
| **Play from beginning** | Ignores your position |
| **Play trailer** | Plays the trailer, when there is one |
| **Mark as watched** / **unwatched** | Sets the tick by hand |
| **Remove from Continue watching** | Takes it out of that row |
| **View details** | Opens the detail page |
| **Add to...** | Puts it in a playlist or collection |
| **Add to watchlist** | Films and shows only, and only while you are online |
| **Download** | Saves it for [offline viewing](/docs/downloads-and-offline) |
| **Play in external player** | Hands it to VLC or similar |

## Rearranging Home

**Settings**, then **Home layout**, decides which rows appear and in what order. Changes
apply immediately rather than after a restart.
