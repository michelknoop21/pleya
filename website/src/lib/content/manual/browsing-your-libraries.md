# Browsing your libraries

The **Media** tab shows one library from your server, say Films or Shows, split into four
tabs.

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Recommended** | Selections the server puts forward from this library |
| **Browse** | Everything, as a grid or a list |
| **Collections** | Collections, such as a complete film series |
| **Playlists** | Playlists |

![A library grid with the letter bar](/docs-media/library-browse.png)

## Finding things in a large library

- **Filter** by genre, year, unwatched, resolution and more. **Clear all** removes them all
  at once.
- **Sort** by any field the server exposes, ascending or descending.
- **The letter bar** jumps straight to a starting letter. It follows the sort order, so it
  only appears when you are sorted alphabetically.
- **Switch library**: long-press the **Media** tab on a phone; pick it in the sidebar on TV.

![The filter panel with two filters active](/docs-media/library-filters.png)

## Grid or list, tight or roomy

**Library density** and **View mode** in [Settings](/docs/settings-reference) change how
much fits on screen. On a large TV a roomier grid reads better from the sofa; on a tablet
the tight one saves scrolling.

## Local folders as a library

A folder on the device itself can be added as a source through **Settings**, then
**Connections**, then **Add local folder**. Pleya matches those files against your Plex or
Jellyfin library by title, year and episode number, which is how they end up with posters
and with watch progress that syncs both ways. Disc images count: an `.iso` and an unpacked
`BDMV` folder both play.
